[[TLDR:closed]]
Large Ising problems do not fit on one device. Once the machine is distributed, arithmetic is rarely the binding cost. Couplings move through a memory hierarchy. Color steps wait at barriers, while boundary spins cross device links. We model those costs separately and calibrate the model across GPUs and streaming hardware. The model tells us when to keep the working set on chip or relax barriers where quality tolerates it. At larger scales, it schedules boundary exchange behind interior computation. The streaming schedule is implemented on a fabricated multi-chip ASIC.
